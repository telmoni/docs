Billing applies to the hosted service, and to each organization rather than
each person: the organization is the unit of billing, and its projects,
members and API keys are covered by its plan. An organization's Owner and
Admins see its plan beside the organization's name in the header's switcher,
and on its **Billing** page (`/{organization}/billing`), which links to the
public **Plans** page (`/plans`). **Billing** is in the organization's
sidebar, before **Settings**, and `g b` reaches it.

## The Hobby plan

Every organization is on the **Hobby** plan, which is free. Hobby has no
usage limits today, and nothing is for sale yet, so no organization is
charged and no payment details are asked for.

## The plans, ahead of sale

The Plans page already posts the three plans: Hobby, free; Pro and Max, each
one flat monthly price; and packs of more capacity on either paid plan.
Beside them it posts Enterprise, a contract. It posts what each plan will hold, what a pack adds, and what happens past each
limit, and says on the page that none of it applies to any organization yet.
Every feature is on every plan, members are never sold, and nothing is
metered: a bill is the plan and the packs an organization holds, and it does
not move on its own.

Anything that goes on sale, and any limit Hobby gains, is listed here as well,
with what Hobby includes and what more of it costs, before it applies to any
organization.

## One Hobby organization

Once Pro and Max are on sale, the organizations a person owns on Hobby take
things in one at a time: one is their Hobby organization, and any other is
**dormant**, as Vercel holds an account to one Hobby team. A dormant
organization keeps its projects, its data and its members, and stays readable,
exportable and deletable, but takes in nothing new: exports to the
OpenTelemetry endpoint, questions to the console agent and invitations are
refused with a `402` that names Pro, until it moves to Pro or Max. Being a
member of other people's organizations is never limited.

- **Your first organization** is your Hobby organization.
- **Another you create** while you own a Hobby organization starts dormant,
  and opens on its **Billing** page, where it moves to Pro or Max. The dialog
  that creates it says so first.
- **A paid organization you cancel** keeps its plan to the end of its period,
  then falls to Hobby: dormant, if you have a Hobby organization by then,
  which the cancel dialog says before you confirm. Until it falls it keeps
  the place for itself, so one you create meanwhile starts dormant, and taking
  the cancellation back frees the place again. A card that fails until its
  retries run out ends the same way.
- **When your Hobby organization leaves Hobby** — deleted, handed to someone
  else, or moved to Pro or Max — the dormant one that has waited longest takes
  its place.
- **Handing an organization over:** one on Hobby, or set to fall to it, goes
  only to an owner with no organization on Hobby or set to fall to it; the
  offer, or its acceptance, is otherwise refused, naming Pro. A paid
  organization goes to anyone.
- **Merging:** a dormant organization's owner can merge it into their Hobby
  organization from its **Billing** page. Every project moves across, with its
  API keys, its data and the people on it, and the emptied organization is
  deleted, confirmed with a code emailed to its owner.

Each change is written to the organization's audit log and raised in its
notifications.

## Self-hosted installations

A self-hosted installation has no plans, no usage limits and no billing.
