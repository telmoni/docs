Short answers, each pointing at the page that binds us. The
[Privacy Policy](/docs/legal/privacy-policy) is the document; this page is a
way into it.

## Who is responsible for my data?

Telmoni LLC, a Missouri limited liability company in Kansas City, Missouri,
United States, provides the hosted service. For your account, billing and
operational data it is the controller. For the content you submit —
your organization's records and the telemetry your agents send — you are the
controller, or a processor for your own customers, and Telmoni acts as your
processor, under the
[data processing terms](/docs/legal/privacy-policy#data-processing-terms) in
the Privacy Policy. Reach us at hello@telmoni.com.

## Where is my data stored?

In the United States, on Google Cloud, in the `us-central1` region. No other
region is offered today. Requests the console agent makes to its model
provider may be processed outside it, as the
[Subprocessors](/docs/legal/subprocessors) page states, and so may the
service's logs.

## Do you train models on my data, or sell it?

No. We do not sell your data, share it for cross-context behavioural
advertising, use it for advertising, or train machine-learning models on it.
The console agent's model provider, on its paid terms, does not train on the
requests either.

## Do you store my prompts and completions?

No. Ingest drops prompts, completions, system instructions, tool inputs and
outputs, retrieved documents, error messages and every attribute that names a
person before anything is stored, on every project: the only content mode
offered today is `off`. The [ingest page](/docs/api/ingest#what-is-dropped-at-the-door)
lists every field, and a project's **Privacy** page in the console shows the
same list beside its content mode.

## What does telemetry hold, then?

Metadata: each run's id, the agent's name, the models and tools it called,
token counts, timings, statuses, an error's type, the names of its steps, the
tags you set, the id you give a session, and the id you give the end user a
run served. The names, tags and session ids are yours to choose:
keep personal data out of them, since they are stored as sent.

## How are end-user ids handled?

The one end-user identifier telemetry keeps, `user.id`, is replaced before it
is stored by its HMAC-SHA-256 under a key made for the project at its first
export, the first 16 bytes of it, and the key is sealed under a managed key
service. The plain id is never stored, a stored hash names nobody outside the
code that sent it, and one project's hashes match no other's. The attributes
that name a person — `user.name`, `user.email`, `user.full_name` — are
dropped.

## How do I delete data, and how fast does it go?

Deleting a project removes it at once and marks every span and hour of totals
it sent deleted in the same purge, so that within moments nothing reads them
again; the nightly maintenance removes them from disk. Deleting an organization closes it at
once and erases it after a 15-minute grace, usually within the hour. Deleting
your account erases your sessions, memberships, exports and your record at
the identity provider. Deleted data then leaves our backups as they expire,
within a further 30 days. [Data deletion](/docs/workspace/data-deletion) says
what each deletion removes; the audit log is the one thing kept through all
of them.

## How long do you keep data?

Telemetry for 360 days after it arrives, and its hourly totals for 13 months,
while the project is active. Agent conversations for 90 days after their last
use, audit log exports for 7 days, operational logs for 30 days, and the
audit log without a time limit. [Data retention](/docs/workspace/data-retention)
has every window.

## Who else processes my data?

Google Cloud for the infrastructure, Stripe for billing, WorkOS for sign-in,
Resend for transactional email, and Google's Gemini API for the console
agent's model and embeddings. Each receives only what its function requires.
The [Subprocessors](/docs/legal/subprocessors) page lists them with their
purpose and location, and we give at least 30 days' notice before adding or
replacing one.

## Is there a data processing agreement?

Yes. The data processing terms in the Privacy Policy form part of the Terms
of Service and apply whenever you process personal data governed by
data-protection law through the service. The European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses, Modules Two and Three, are incorporated for transfers
out of the EU, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum for the UK, and
the clauses as adapted to Swiss law for Switzerland. If your procurement
process needs a countersigned agreement, write to hello@telmoni.com.

## Do you use cookies or analytics?

Only strictly necessary cookies: to keep you signed in, to remember the
organization you are working in, and one to remember a collapsed sidebar.
There are no advertising cookies or third-party trackers, and no
product-analytics provider is configured: usage events stay in our own logs.

## Can I export my data?

The audit log exports as JSON or CSV from the organization's **Audit log**
page, and the API reads your organization's identity and a project's roster.
Telemetry has no export today; the console reads it as traces,
sessions and users.

## What are my rights, and how do I exercise them?

In the console: see and change your account and organization information,
revoke API keys, and delete your account or an organization you own. Beyond
that, depending on where you live, you may have rights under the GDPR or US
state privacy laws to confirm, access, correct, delete or port your personal
information; write to hello@telmoni.com, and we will not discriminate against
you for asking. In the EU or UK you may also complain to your data-protection
authority.

## Will you tell me about a breach?

Yes: without undue delay after we become aware of a personal-data breach
affecting your personal data, with enough information for you to meet your
own notification obligations.

## Can children use the service?

No. The service is not directed to children under 16, and you must be at
least 18 to use it.
