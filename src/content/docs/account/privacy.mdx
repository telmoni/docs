title: "Privacy and deletion"
description: "Managing active sessions, product analytics, organization restoration, and permanent account deletion."
---

Your account's **Privacy** page (`/account/privacy`) manages session security,
analytics preferences, data retention, and account lifecycle.

## Active sessions

The **Active sessions** panel lists all devices currently authenticated to your
account, including device information, operating system, IP address, and last
active timestamp.

You can terminate any remote session individually. To terminate your current
session, sign out from the user menu.

## Product analytics

**Disabled by default.** When enabled, Telmoni collects basic operational page
navigation events to identify errors and UX friction points. It never collects
API tokens, credentials, or workspace payloads.

## Deleting an organization

Only an organization **Owner** can delete an organization. Deletion is initiated
from the **Danger zone** in **Organization Settings**
(`/organization/settings`):

1. Request a 6-digit confirmation code, which is emailed to your address and
   remains valid for 15 minutes.
2. Enter the confirmation code to confirm deletion.
3. Access ends immediately for all members, and all API keys stop functioning.

### Restoring a deleted organization

For **14 days** following deletion (`RESTORE_WINDOW_SECONDS`), the organization
remains in a soft-deleted state (`pending_deletion`). During this window:

- The organization appears under **Deleted organizations** on the Owner's
  **Privacy** page (`/account/privacy`).
- Selecting **Restore** reactivates the organization, restoring all projects,
  members, API keys, and notification channels exactly as they were.

After 14 days have elapsed, the automated deletion sweep permanently erases the
organization, its projects, and all associated data.

## Deleting your account

Account deletion is permanent and performed from the **Danger zone** on your
account's **Privacy** page (`/account/privacy`).

### Ownership pre-check

**Account deletion is blocked if you are the Owner of an organization containing
other members.** You must either:
- [Transfer ownership](/workspace/organizations-and-projects/#transferring-organization-ownership)
  to an Admin.
- Remove all other members from the organization.
- Or delete the organization from its settings page.

### Deletion procedure

1. Select **Delete account** and request an email confirmation code.
2. Enter the 6-digit confirmation code (valid for 15 minutes).
3. Confirm permanent deletion.

### What happens when your account is deleted

- **Organizations you exclusively own**: Marked for immediate deletion and
  permanently purged. There is no 14-day restore window for organizations
  destroyed via account deletion.
- **Other organizations you belong to**: You are removed from all other
  organizations and projects. Workspace audit logs record your departure.
- **Notifications**: Feed notices referencing your account are anonymized
  (e.g., "A former member joined").
- **Authentication**: Your password hash or identity provider association is
  erased, your user record is purged, and all active sessions are terminated
  immediately.
