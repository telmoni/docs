---
title: "Account settings"
description: "Managing your display name, changing your account email address, resetting your password, and interface preferences."
---

Your account's **Settings** page (`/account/settings`) manages personal account
credentials. These settings belong to your identity alone and apply across all
organizations you belong to.

## Your display name

Your display name is set during initial registration or provided by your identity
provider. It appears on comments, invitations, and audit log entries attributed
to you.

## Changing your email address

Accounts that authenticate using email and password can change their registered
email address. (Accounts using external identity providers must update their
email directly with their provider).

Updating your email requires two 6-digit verification codes:

1. Enter the new email address, which must not already be registered.
2. Two 6-digit codes are dispatched simultaneously:
   - One to your **current** email address.
   - One to your **new** email address.
3. Enter both verification codes into the console. Both codes expire after
   **15 minutes**.

When the email update succeeds:
- **All active sessions are terminated immediately** (including the current
  session). You must sign in again with your new email address.
- Any pending deletion codes or sensitive confirmation codes issued to the old
  email address are invalidated.

## Resetting your password

For email/password accounts, select **Email me a password reset link**. A single-use
token is dispatched to your email address, valid for **60 minutes**. Using the
reset link invalidates all existing sessions and updates the stored Argon2id
password hash.

## Interface preferences

The user menu in the console header configures display preferences for your
browser session:

- **Theme**: Select **System theme** (synchronizes with your operating system's
  appearance), **Light theme**, or **Dark theme**.
- **Corners**: Configure UI geometry between **Rounded corners** and **Sharp corners**.
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: Press `?` or select **Keyboard shortcuts** from the
  user menu to inspect available shortcuts and toggle single-key navigation.
