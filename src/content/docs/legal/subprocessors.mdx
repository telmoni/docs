To provide the hosted platform, Telmoni LLC engages the providers below
("subprocessors"), each of which may process personal data on our behalf.
Each receives only what its function requires and is bound to use it only to
provide its service to us. This list is part of the
[Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/), and your use of the service
authorizes these subprocessors.

| Provider | Entity | Purpose | Data | Primary location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Google Cloud | Google LLC | Cloud infrastructure: the application runtime, database and cache, key management, secret storage and container registry | Everything the service stores | United States |
| Stripe | Stripe, Inc. | Billing and payment processing. We never store a full payment-card number; Stripe handles card data under its own terms | Billing details, invoice history | United States |
| WorkOS | WorkOS, Inc. | Sign-in, identity and single sign-on | Authentication identifiers such as name and email address | United States |
| Resend | Resend, Inc. | Transactional email: invitations, ownership-transfer offers and deletion confirmation codes | The recipient's address and the message contents | United States |

Where product analytics is configured, usage events for the activation funnel
may be sent to a product-analytics provider, keyed to a pseudonymous
identifier rather than to your content. When it is not configured, these
events stay in our own logs and no third party receives them. This
subprocessor is optional and enabled per deployment; it will be named here
when it is.

## Changes

We will give at least 30 days' notice before adding or replacing a
subprocessor by updating this page and, if you have subscribed to updates, by
email. During that period you may object on reasonable data-protection
grounds; if we cannot reasonably accommodate the objection, your remedy is to
stop using the affected part of the service and, if that is not workable, to
terminate. Where a change is urgently required to keep the service running,
we may make it and notify you as soon as practicable, and your objection right
still applies.
