A Slack or Discord connector posts project [notifications](/integrations/notifications/)
directly into a specified channel.

Connectors are configured per project. Project **Owners** and **Admins** can
connect and manage destinations from the project's **Connectors** page
(`/[projectId]/connectors`). Project **Members** can view active connectors and
their delivery histories.

## Connecting a channel

1. Open your project's **Connectors** page.
2. Select **Connect** next to Slack or Discord.
3. You will be directed to Slack or Discord's authorization screen. Choose the
   destination workspace and target channel.
4. Once authorized, you are redirected back to the **Connectors** page, and the
   channel appears active.

Authorization handshakes must be completed within 10 minutes.

## Permissions and security

Telmoni requests the minimal permissions required to post inbound messages:
- **Slack**: `incoming-webhook`
- **Discord**: `webhook.incoming`

These permissions restrict Telmoni to posting messages into the exact channel
selected. Telmoni cannot view chat messages, inspect other channels, or enumerate
workspace members. All access credentials and webhook targets are stored
encrypted at rest.

## Delivery and retries

Notices are sent immediately upon creation. If a delivery attempt fails due to a
transient network error or provider rate limit:

- Telmoni retries delivery up to **5 times** using exponential backoff with
  jitter, up to a maximum delay of 24 hours.
- If the provider responds with a `Retry-After` header, Telmoni respects the
  requested delay.

## Disconnection and breaker behavior

If a destination endpoint is permanently removed (e.g., the channel is deleted,
the incoming webhook is removed, or the Telmoni application is uninstalled from
the workspace), Telmoni marks the connector as disconnected and dispatches a
`connector_disconnected` event to the workspace feed.

Removing the Telmoni application from a Slack workspace disconnects all channels
connected to that Slack workspace. The event is recorded in the project's
[audit log](/workspace/audit-log/) with Slack listed as the actor.

## Disconnecting manually

To remove a connection, select **Disconnect** on the connector's row on the
**Connectors** page. Telmoni invalidates the local grant, informs the upstream
vendor, and logs the action in the audit log.
