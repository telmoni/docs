---
title: "Notifications"
description: "What Telmoni records in activity feeds, where notices are displayed, and how notifications are dispatched to external destinations."
---

A notification is an event record describing an action or status update within
a workspace: a member joining, or a connector state change. Every notice is
stored durably in Telmoni before being dispatched to downstream destinations.

## Notice types

| Kind | Raised when |
|---|---|
| `member_added` | Someone accepts an invitation to the project |
| `member_left` | A member leaves a project or is removed from it, including when they leave or are removed from its organization |
| `connector_connected` | A Slack, Discord, or webhook connector finishes setup |
| `connector_disconnected` | A connection stops: the vendor reports the channel or installation gone, a webhook answers `410` or fails ten sends in a row, or the Telmoni app is removed from a Slack workspace |
| `organization_alert` | An alert for the organization's Owner, raised by services built on the open core; the core itself raises none |

## Where you read them

- **Project feed**: The **Recent activity** section of each project's
  **Overview** page surfaces project notices. Every project role can read the
  feed.
- **Organization feed**: The organization's **Overview** page shows the
  organization's own notices (`organization_alert`) to its Owner and Admins.
- **Invitations and offers**: Invitations to you, and offers of an organization
  or a project, appear separately in the console header bell and on your
  account's **Notifications** page (`/account/notifications`).

Selecting **Mark read** marks every unread notice in that feed as read, for
everyone who reads it.

## Forwarding to external connectors

Projects can forward notifications to third-party endpoints through connectors
configured on the project's **Connectors** page
(`/{organization}/{project}/connectors`):

- **[Slack and Discord](/integrations/slack-and-discord/)**: Direct channel
  notifications for workspace awareness.
- **[Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks/)**: HTTPS POST requests with HMAC-SHA256
  signatures delivered directly to your own infrastructure.

Connectors deliver notices asynchronously. A temporary failure or network outage
on an external service retries without losing the underlying notification record.

## Retention

- **Feed notices**: Retained for **90 days** (`FEED_RETENTION_DAYS`).
- **Connector delivery logs**: Kept for **30 days** (`DELIVERY_RETENTION_DAYS`).
