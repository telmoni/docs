---
title: "Notifications"
description: "What Telmoni records in activity feeds, where notices are displayed, and how notifications are dispatched to external destinations."
---

A notification is an event record describing an action or status update within
a workspace: a member joining, or a connector state change. Every notice is
stored durably in Telmoni before being dispatched to downstream destinations.

## Notice types

| Kind | Raised when |
|---|---|
| `member_added` | A user accepts an invitation to the workspace |
| `connector_connected` | A Slack, Discord, or webhook connector finishes setup |
| `connector_disconnected` | A connector destination becomes unreachable or repeatedly fails |
| `organization_alert` | An operational alert directed to the organization Owner |

## Where you read them

- **Project feed**: The **Recent activity** section of each project's
  **Overview** page surfaces project notices. Project Admins and Members can
  read the feed.
- **Organization feed**: The organization's **Overview** page displays
  organization-level notices and alerts.
- **Invitations**: Direct workspace invitations appear separately in the console
  header bell and on your account's **Notifications** page
  (`/account/notifications`).

Selecting **Mark read** marks notices as read across the feed view.

## Forwarding to external connectors

Projects can forward notifications to third-party endpoints through connectors
configured on the project's **Connectors** page (`/[projectId]/connectors`):

- **[Slack and Discord](/integrations/slack-and-discord/)**: Direct channel
  notifications for workspace awareness.
- **[Webhooks](/integrations/webhooks/)**: HTTPS POST requests with HMAC-SHA256
  signatures delivered directly to your own infrastructure.

Connectors deliver notices asynchronously. A temporary failure or network outage
on an external service retries without losing the underlying notification record.

## Retention

- **Feed notices**: Retained for **90 days** (`FEED_RETENTION_DAYS`).
- **Connector delivery logs**: Kept for **30 days** (`DELIVERY_RETENTION_DAYS`).
