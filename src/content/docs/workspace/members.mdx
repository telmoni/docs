---
title: "Members and invitations"
description: "Inviting people to an organization or project, accepting and declining, changing roles, removing members, and leaving."
---

Collaborators join an organization or project by invitation. An invitation grants
a [role](/workspace/roles/) of **Admin** or **Member**.

## Scope of an invitation

Telmoni supports two invitation scopes:

1. **Organization invitations**: Invited from the organization's **Members** page
   (`/organization/members`). Grants an organization role (`Admin` or `Member`).
   An invited Organization Admin gains administrative access across all projects
   in the organization.
2. **Project invitations**: Invited from a project's **Members** page
   (`/[projectId]/members`). Grants access directly to that project as an
   `Admin` or `Member` without granting organization-wide permissions.

## Inviting someone

An Owner or Admin can send invitations:

1. Navigate to the **Members** page of the organization or project.
2. Enter the recipient's email address and choose a role (**Admin** or
   **Member**).
3. Telmoni emails an invitation link, and the console displays the link once so
   you can share it directly.

**An invitation is created for any email address, whether or not they currently
hold a Telmoni account.** Inviting someone never discloses whether the address is
already registered.

Invitations expire automatically after **7 days** (`OFFER_TTL_DAYS`).

## Accepting or declining

Invitations can be answered in two places:

- **The console bell & notifications**: Pending invitations appear in the bell icon
  in the console header and on your account's **Notifications** page
  (`/account/notifications`). You can accept or decline directly without
  leaving your current view.
- **The email link**: Opens a verification view showing the organization or project
  and the role granted. You must sign in or register with the exact email address
  the invitation was sent to.

Accepting an invitation records an audit event and posts a `member_added` notice
to the workspace feed. Declining an invitation removes it immediately.

## Withdrawing a pending invitation

An Owner or Admin can withdraw a pending invitation at any time from the
**Members** page by selecting **Withdraw** on its row. Withdrawn links are
invalidated immediately.

## Changing roles and removing members

An Owner or Admin can change a member's role or remove them from the
**Members** page:

- **Role changes**: Changing between Admin and Member takes effect immediately
  and is recorded in the audit log.
- **Removing members**: Removing a member revokes their access immediately.
- **The Owner cannot be removed or demoted**: The Owner's row is marked **Owner**
  and carries no removal controls. To change the Owner, the current Owner must
  initiate an [ownership transfer](/workspace/organizations-and-projects/#transferring-organization-ownership).

## Leaving an organization or project

- Members and Admins can leave a project or organization from the **Members**
  page or workspace overview.
- The Owner cannot leave until ownership is transferred to an Admin or the
  organization is deleted.

## Invitation limits

An organization can send up to **20 invitations in any 24 hours** (including
withdrawn ones). There is no hard limit on the total number of members in an
organization.
