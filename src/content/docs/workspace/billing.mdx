---
title: "Billing"
description: "What the hosted service charges for: every organization is on the Hobby plan, which has no usage limits and no charge."
---

Billing applies to the hosted service, and to each organization rather than
each person: the organization is the unit of billing, and its projects,
members and API keys are covered by its plan. An organization's Owner and
Admins see its plan on its **Billing** page (`/{organization}/~/billing`).

## The Hobby plan

Every organization is on the **Hobby** plan. Hobby has no usage limits, and
nothing is for sale yet, so no organization is charged and no payment details
are asked for.

Anything that goes on sale is listed on this page, with what Hobby includes
of it and what more of it costs, before any organization can be charged
for it.

## Self-hosted installations

A self-hosted installation has no plans, no usage limits and no billing.
